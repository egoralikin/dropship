## What I need from you, in priority order

### Check A — CJ sourcing

**A1 first, before anything else.** Ask CJ support directly:

> *"If a customer orders 6 different SKUs in one order, do you combine them into one parcel and charge one
> shipping fee, or charge shipping per item?"*

This one answer swings Bundle A's break-even ROAS from 1.63× to roughly 2.2×. It matters more than every
other number. Record the answer and where it came from.

**A2 — per component**, for each item in Bundles A and B, search CJdropshipping and record:

CJ product URL · unit cost USD · **US warehouse stock Y/N** · shipping method to US with cost and stated
days · listing rating · total order count · MOQ · packaging (neutral or branded)

**Filter for US-warehouse stock specifically.** China-direct is 10–20 days; US warehouse is 2–5. My
under-10-day rule effectively requires US warehouse. Flag any listing under 100 orders or below 4.5 rating.

For the cable tray and the felt mat specifically, also find a **backup supplier** — those two items decide
whether the kit feels cheap and whether "wool" is an honest word.

**On the felt mat:** record exactly what the listing claims about fibre content, including wool percentage
if stated. I will not use the word "wool" in any product copy unless the listing and a physical sample
agree. If it's polyester, it's "felt".

### Check B — Amazon / Temu price floor (this one can kill the plan)

For each component, the cheapest equivalent. Then the question that actually matters:

**Does a complete desk cable-management kit at $60–80 already exist on Amazon?**

Give me the top 10 results for "desk cable management kit" with price, review count, rating, and whether
each is a single item or a multi-piece kit — then the cheapest complete multi-piece kit and its contents.

**A comparable complete kit at $40 or under on Prime ends Bundle A.** Tell me plainly if you find one.

### Check C — Meta Ad Library (best free demand signal)

`facebook.com/ads/library` → Ad category **All ads** → Country **United States**.
Search: `cable management`, `desk cable`, `cable tray`, `desk mat`, `felt desk mat`, `desk setup`.

Per advertiser: page name · active ad count · **earliest ad start date** · creative format (UGC / demo /
before-after / static) · landing page price · single item or kit.

**Ad start date is the signal** — nobody funds a losing ad for two months, so 60+ days running means a
validated offer. Long-running ads at $60–80 kit prices would confirm my price point. A field of only
single-item ads around $25 means the kit angle is unproven, which is either the opening or the warning
depending on what Check B says.

### Check D — Google Trends
US, 12 months: `cable management`, `desk mat`, `desk setup`. Stable/rising or collapsing? Note Q4
seasonality — desk gear is a gifting category and Q4 is close.

### Check E — Review mining
On the closest Amazon equivalents for the cable tray and the felt desk mat, filter **1–2 star reviews** and
give me the top 3 complaint themes each. I expect adhesive failure on trays and fibre/smell/thickness on
mats. This sets my refund reserve and tells me what the product copy has to address honestly rather than
hide.

### Check F — TikTok Creative Center (optional)
Organic demand cross-check for the same terms.
