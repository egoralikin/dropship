Standalone. Paste everything below the rule.

## Context

US dropshipping store, pre-launch, nothing spent. I need live browser research to validate one product
before I commit any money.

**The product:** a "Desk Set" bundle sold at **$75** — wool-blend felt desk mat (80×40cm), felt mousepad,
2 felt coasters, magnetic cord catch, felt pen tray. Sourced from CJdropshipping, shipped to US customers.
Estimated landed cost $23.10, break-even ROAS 1.58×.

**Why I'm careful:** I just killed my previous hero product — a $69 desk cable-management kit — because
research found Amazon Prime already sells a ~$15 accessory kit and 26-piece tray kits. The bundle thesis
("a kit isn't price-comparable to a single SKU") turned out to be false there, because Amazon already sold
the kit. **I need the same test run against this product before it inherits hero status.** Please try to
kill it.

## Check 1 — Amazon / Temu price floor *(the kill test — do this first)*

Search Amazon US for: `felt desk mat set`, `desk mat set`, `felt desk pad`, `desk accessories set felt`.
Also check Temu for the equivalent.

Report the top 10 for each with **price, review count, rating, and whether it's a single mat or a
multi-piece set** — then answer three questions directly:

1. **Does a comparable multi-piece felt desk set sell at $40 or less on Prime?**
   → If yes, this product is dead. Say so plainly.
2. **What does a single felt desk mat of similar size (roughly 80×40cm) cost on Prime?**
   → If good ones are at or under $25, tell me. The set then has to justify +$50 on presentation alone,
   which is a brand bet rather than a price bet, and I want to know I'm making it.
3. **Do premium felt/leather desk mats exist well above $75** (Grovemade, Oakywood, Orbitkey, similar)?
   → This is the supportive case, so please look for it as carefully as the kill case: if $80–150 desk
   mats sell, $75 is an established price point and my positioning is "premium look, fair price."

## Check 2 — CJdropshipping sourcing

**2a — same-warehouse requirement.** CJ only combines an order into one parcel if **all SKUs sit in the
same warehouse**. So: can all 5 components be sourced from **one CJ US warehouse**? If the mat ships from
a US warehouse but the coasters ship from China, the bundle pays two shipments and two delivery windows,
and my under-10-day promise breaks. This is a hard requirement — please check it explicitly per component,
not in general.

**2b — oversized fees.** An 80×40cm mat rolled for shipping is exactly the shape that attracts oversized
or volumetric charges. Get me: the mat's **rolled dimensions and weight**, CJ's **oversized threshold**,
and the **fee** if it's exceeded. An extra $8 here takes my break-even ROAS from 1.58× to 1.90× and this
stops being worth doing, so it's the number I most need.

**2c — fulfilment fees.** From `cjdropshipping.com/service-fee`, get the **actual** per-order fulfilment
fee and the per-extra-item charge. I'm estimating $1.10 total for a 5-item bundle; I want the real figures.

**2d — per component** (mat, mousepad, coasters, cord catch, pen tray), record:
CJ product URL · unit cost · US warehouse Y/N · shipping cost and stated days · listing rating · order
count · MOQ · packaging (neutral or branded).
Flag anything under 100 orders or below 4.5 rating. Find a **backup supplier for the mat** specifically.

**2e — fibre content.** For each mat listing, record exactly what's claimed about fibre, including wool
percentage if stated. I will not use the word "wool" in any copy unless the listing and a physical sample
agree. If it's polyester it gets described as felt, at the same price.

## Check 3 — Meta Ad Library

`facebook.com/ads/library` → Ad category **All ads** → Country **United States**.
Search: `desk mat`, `felt desk mat`, `desk pad`, `desk setup`, `leather desk mat`.

Per advertiser: page name · active ad count · **earliest ad start date** · creative format (UGC / demo /
flat-lay / static) · landing page price · single item or set.

**Ad start date is the signal** — nobody funds a losing ad for two months, so 60+ days running means a
validated offer. I specifically want to know whether anyone is running long-lived ads for a desk mat
**set** at $60–80, or whether the whole field is single mats at $25–35.

## Check 4 — Review mining

On the closest Amazon equivalents for felt desk mats, filter **1–2 star reviews** and give me the top 3
complaint themes. I expect fibre/smell/thickness/shedding and curling at the edges. This sets my refund
reserve (currently estimated at 2.5%, which may be optimistic) and tells me what my product copy has to
address honestly rather than hide.
